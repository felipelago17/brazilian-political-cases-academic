## Guarujá triplex {#lula-guaruja-triplex}

**Acusación.** Bribery and money laundering via an OAS beach apartment; STJ fixed reparation at R$2.42m.

**Cronología.**

- 2017-07-12 — Convicted at first instance (Judge Moro)
- 2018-01-24 — Conviction upheld and sentence increased by TRF-4
- 2021-03-08 — STF (Fachin): Curitiba court lacked jurisdiction; conviction annulled
- 2021-03-23 — STF Second Panel finds Judge Moro biased
- 2022-01 — Case restarted in Brasília; archived on prescription (halved for age over 70)

**Registro oficial.** HC 193726 (jurisdiction); HC 164493 (bias) — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). Cómo obtenerlo: Search by HC number on the STF portal; decisions are public and downloadable from the process page.

**Notas.** No merits judgment survives; the conviction was annulled on jurisdiction and the restarted case expired.

## Atibaia country house {#lula-atibaia}

**Acusación.** Bribery and money laundering; c. R$1m of refurbishments paid by construction firms.

**Cronología.**

- 2019-02-06 — Convicted at first instance
- 2021-03-08 — Annulled by STF with the other Curitiba cases
- 2025-07 — TRF-1 refuses reopening; time limit expired

**Registro oficial.** HC 193726 — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). Cómo obtenerlo: Same STF decision as the triplex case; TRF-1 decision via consulta processual.

## Instituto Lula land {#lula-instituto-land}

**Acusación.** Odebrecht bribe in the form of land for the Institute (c. R$12m).

**Cronología.**

- 2021-03-08 — Annulled with the Curitiba cases
- 2023-02-17 — Closed by Justice Lewandowski: Odebrecht leniency-deal evidence declared unusable

**Registro oficial.** Lewandowski decision of 17 Feb 2023 — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). Cómo obtenerlo: Consulta processual by party name; decision published on the STF portal.

**Cronología.**

- 2023-02-17 — Closed on the same Lewandowski decision

**Registro oficial.** Lewandowski decision of 17 Feb 2023 — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). Cómo obtenerlo: As above.

## Obstruction of justice (Cerveró / Delcídio) {#lula-obstruction-cervero}

**Acusación.** Scheme to buy the silence of former Petrobras director Nestor Cerveró.

**Cronología.**

- 2018-07-12 — Acquitted on the merits (Judge Ricardo Leite): insufficient evidence; prosecutors themselves requested acquittal

**Registro oficial.** Sentence of 12 Jul 2018, 10th Federal Criminal Court, DF — [portal](https://portal.trf1.jus.br/). Cómo obtenerlo: Consulta processual on the TRF-1 portal.

**Notas.** The only Lula case in this set decided on the merits.

## Coup plot (AP 2668) {#bolsonaro-coup-ap2668}

**Acusación.** Attempted coup d'état, violent abolition of the democratic rule of law, armed criminal organisation, and related counts.

**Cronología.**

- 2025-09-11 — Convicted 4–1; 27 years 3 months
- 2026-03-24 — Humanitarian house arrest granted
- 2026-10 — Criminal-review petitions pending; PGR opposes

**Registro oficial.** AP 2668 — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). Cómo obtenerlo: Search AP 2668 on the STF portal; judgment and acórdão are public.

**Notas.** See foreign-actions.yaml for the US measures that bracket this judgment, and linkages.yaml for how they are graded.

## Coercion in the course of proceedings (AP 2782) {#eduardo-bolsonaro-coercion-ap2782}

**Acusación.** Used his office as federal deputy to seek US sanctions against Brazilian authorities in order to influence proceedings.

**Cronología.**

- 2026-06 — Unanimous conviction; 4 years 2 months
- 2026-10-03 — Judgment final
- 2026-10-08 — Arrest warrant issued; defendant resident in the US since Feb 2025

**Registro oficial.** AP 2782 — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). Cómo obtenerlo: Search AP 2782 on the STF portal.

**Notas.** The judgment itself documents the connection between the defendant's conduct and the US measures recorded in foreign-actions.yaml — see linkages.yaml.
