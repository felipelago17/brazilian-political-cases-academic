# Source hierarchy

Primary sources are preferred. Secondary sources are labelled and never the sole basis for a linkage grade.

## Tier 1 — Primary (official)

- Supreme Federal Court (STF) — process pages, DJe, acórdãos; Superior Court of Justice (STJ); Superior Electoral Court (TSE) and regional electoral courts; Federal Regional Courts (TRF-1 to TRF-6); state courts
- Prosecutor-General (PGR) and Federal Prosecution Service (MPF) — denúncias, pareceres, agreements
- Federal Court of Accounts (TCU) acórdãos; Comptroller-General (CGU) — PAR decisions, CEIS/CNEP/CEPIM lists; Attorney-General (AGU); CADE; COAF public statements
- Diário Oficial da União (DOU); Diário da Justiça Eletrônico (DJe)
- Câmara dos Deputados and Senado Federal open-data APIs, committee pages, roll calls
- Portal da Transparência; TSE DivulgaCand
- Foreign official sources: US Federal Register, OFAC, DOJ, SEC, State Department; EU Official Journal

## Tier 2 — Official secondary

- Agência Brasil (EBC); Agência Câmara; Agência Senado; court and prosecutor press offices
- Government, court or legislative statements later formalised in Tier 1 documents

## Tier 3 — Labelled secondary

- Mainstream and legal press (Folha, Estadão, O Globo, Valor, Conjur, Migalhas, JOTA, Poder360, Gazeta do Povo, BBC Brasil, Reuters, AP, Bloomberg) — always cited as secondary
- Law-firm client alerts and think-tank commentary — always cited as secondary

## Excluded

Openly partisan or campaigning outlets, on any side, are not used as sources. Where an excluded outlet is the only public record of a fact, the fact is omitted until a Tier 1–3 source exists.

## Rules

- A `statutory-mandate` or `documented-request` grade requires at least one Tier 1 source.
- `proximity-only` may cite Tier 2 or Tier 3 but must state that no inference is drawn.
- Every tracker entry must include at least one URL and its tier.
- Official decisions are linked directly whenever the court publishes a stable URL (STF process pages, DJe PDFs, TSE decisions). Where a portal requires a search, the entry states the case number and the retrieval path.
