## 2025-07-30 — US Treasury / OFAC {#ofac-moraes-designation-2025-07}

**Instrumento:** Global Magnitsky designation (SDN List)

**Alvo:** Alexandre de Moraes (STF Justice) · **Em vigor:** revogada 2025-12-12

First use of the Global Magnitsky Act against a Brazilian public official; assets in US jurisdiction blocked and US persons barred from dealing with him.

**Fontes.** [OFAC recent actions](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions) (N1) · [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/us-sanctions-brazil-supreme-court-justice-overseeing-bolsonaro-trial/) (N3) · [Barnes & Thornburg](https://btlaw.com/en/insights/alerts/2025/us-sanctions-brazilian-supreme-court-justice-places-50-tariff-on-brazilian-imports) (N3)

## 2025-07-30 — US President (IEEPA) {#us-brazil-tariff-ieepa-2025-07}

**Instrumento:** Executive Order 14323, *Addressing Threats to the United States by the Government of Brazil*, 90 FR 37739 — additional 40 percent duty on Brazil-origin goods (50 percent total with the reciprocal tariff; Section 232 goods excluded)

**Alvo:** Brazil (trade) · **Em vigor:** revogada 2026-02-24

The order's stated grounds included the treatment of former President Bolsonaro. Invalidated as a tariff measure by Learning Resources v. Trump (20 Feb 2026), which held IEEPA does not authorise tariffs; IEEPA duties ended by EO 14389 effective 24 Feb 2026.

**Fontes.** [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/05/2025-14896/addressing-threats-to-the-united-states-by-the-government-of-brazil) (N1) · [Supreme Court](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/607/24-1287/) (N1) · [Mondaq / Barnes & Thornburg](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/export-controls-trade-investment-sanctions/1662270/us-sanctions-brazilian-supreme-court-justice-places-50-tariff-on-brazilian-imports) (N3)
