## Guarujá triplex {#lula-guaruja-triplex}

**Persons:** Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva (PT) · **Origin:** Lava Jato (Curitiba) · **Forum:** 13th Federal Court of Curitiba → TRF-4 → STF

**Stage / Outcome:** closed / archived · **Mechanisms:** incompetência, suspeição, prescrição

**Allegation.** Bribery and money laundering via an OAS beach apartment; STJ fixed reparation at R$2.42m.

**Timeline.**

- 2017-07-12 — Convicted at first instance (Judge Moro)
- 2018-01-24 — Conviction upheld and sentence increased by TRF-4
- 2021-03-08 — STF (Fachin): Curitiba court lacked jurisdiction; conviction annulled
- 2021-03-23 — STF Second Panel finds Judge Moro biased
- 2022-01 — Case restarted in Brasília; archived on prescription (halved for age over 70)

**Official record.** HC 193726 (jurisdiction); HC 164493 (bias) — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). How to retrieve: Search by HC number on the STF portal; decisions are public and downloadable from the process page.

**Notes.** No merits judgment survives; the conviction was annulled on jurisdiction and the restarted case expired.

**Sources.** [Gazeta SP](https://gazetasp.com.br/politica/lula-x-flavio-bolsonaro-e-seus-processos-na-justica-dois-candidatos-a-presidencia-zero-julgamento-de-merito) (T3) · [BBC Brasil via O Povo](https://www.opovo.com.br/agencia/bbc/2026/10/22/lulinha-luchsinger-e-lava-jato-o-que-realmente-pesa-contra-lula.html) (T3)

**Official record.** AP 2782 — [portal](https://portal.stf.jus.br/processos/). How to retrieve: Search AP 2782 on the STF portal.
