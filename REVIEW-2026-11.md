# Review and expansion roadmap — November 2026

**Scope of this review.** Assessment of the repository as it stands (commit `adfe763`, status date 31 October 2026) against the architecture and methodology used in the companion repository `Congressional-Influence-Sanctions-Export-Controls`, with a proposal to expand from *legal cases* to *accountability matters* more broadly. Nothing here changes the repository's stated neutrality: it records what public sources report, applies the presumption of innocence, and draws no inference.

---

The strengths are real and should be kept: the "official availability + how to retrieve" field is a genuinely useful design; the methodology statement is sound; the related-open-data section (Serenata, Querido Diário, FiscalizaJá) is the right instinct about an indicators layer; the CC BY licence and trilingual ambition fit an academic audience.

## 2. What to carry over from the Congressional repository

Five design decisions transfer directly.

1. **YAML trackers as the single source of truth, pages generated from them.** One `trackers/*.yaml` per subject; a build script renders EN/PT/ES tables. Labels are translated once in a dictionary; free-text fields are authored in Portuguese (the language of the sources) with an English `summary_en`. This ends translation drift.
2. **Source hierarchy with tiers, and a rule that grades require Tier 1.** Brazilian Tier 1: STF/STJ/TSE/TRF/TCU/CGU/PGR/MPF/CADE/COAF official pages, Diário Oficial da União, DJe, Câmara/Senado open data, Portal da Transparência; Tier 2: Agência Brasil, Agência Câmara/Senado, court press offices; Tier 3: press and law-firm commentary, labelled.
3. **Multi-status fields instead of free text.** For a case: `stage` from a fixed taxonomy (inquérito → denúncia → recebida → instrução → sentença → recurso → trânsito em julgado) crossed with `outcome` (condenação / absolvição / anulação / prescrição / arquivamento / pendente) and `mechanism` (incompetência, suspeição, nulidade probatória, prescrição, revisão criminal, anistia…). For a regulatory or legislative item: scheduled / announced / published / in force.
4. **Signals and actions, linked only with a grade.** The Brazilian analogue of "congressional signal → administration action" is **political or foreign signal → institutional action**: a bill, a CPI, an impeachment request, a US sanction, a tariff → a court decision, a TCU ruling, a CGU sanction, a delisting. Grades: `statutory-mandate` (the law required it), `documented-request` (the acting body cites the signal in its own document), `proximity-only` (no inference drawn). This is the single most important addition: it lets the repository record, for example, that the Moraes Magnitsky designation (30 Jul 2025), his wife's (22 Sep 2025) and the delisting (12 Dec 2025) bracket the Bolsonaro conviction (11 Sep 2025) and the Trump–Lula rapprochement — **as dates and documents, without asserting cause**.
5. **Automated feeds with a "targets" mechanism**, so that every "official decision to be added" resolves itself the next morning, and a daily digest page shows what changed.

## 3. Scope expansion — from cases to matters

Proposed trackers, each with its Tier 1 source and whether it can be automated now.

| Tracker | What it records | Tier 1 source | Automation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cases.yaml` (existing, migrated) | Criminal/civil/electoral cases against public figures, all parties | STF, STJ, TSE, TRF portals; DJe | Partial — CNJ **DataJud public API** returns case metadata (class, subject, movements) by process number |
| `institutional-actions.yaml` | TCU acórdãos, CGU sanctions (CEIS/CNEP/CEPIM lists), CADE decisions, COAF-related public actions, leniency agreements (CGU/AGU/MPF) | TCU search; **Portal da Transparência API** (free key) for CEIS/CNEP; CADE SEI | Yes — Portal da Transparência; TCU via Querido Diário/DOU |
| `legislative-signals.yaml` | Bills and PECs on STF powers, judicial review, amnesty, foro privilegiado, electoral law; CPIs/CPMIs; impeachment requests against justices (Senate); Senate votes on judicial nominations | **Câmara dos Deputados API** and **Senado API** (no key); Senado impeachment-request list | Yes — both APIs are open; keyword scan mirrors the Congress.gov script |
| `foreign-actions.yaml` | US and other foreign actions touching Brazilian public figures or trade: OFAC designations/delistings, visa revocations, Section 232/301, the 50 percent IEEPA tariff (EO of July 2025, now void after *Learning Resources v. Trump*, 20 Feb 2026), DOJ/SEC FCPA resolutions involving Brazilian companies (Odebrecht/Braskem, Petrobras, Embraer…) | OFAC recent actions; Federal Register; DOJ/SEC releases | Yes — reuse the Federal Register and agency-releases scrapers already built, filtered on "Brazil" |
| `elections.yaml` | 2026 cycle: ineligibilities (Bolsonaro until 2030), candidacies, TSE rulings, campaign-finance matters, disinformation decisions | TSE DivulgaCand and jurisprudence; TSE news | Partial — DivulgaCand API is open |
| `regulatory.yaml` | Enforcement and rule changes under Lei 12.846 (Anticorrupção), Lei 14.133 (Licitações), Lei 9.613 (Lavagem), Lei 8.429 (Improbidade, as amended 2021); CGU PAR statistics | DOU; CGU | Partial — DOU via **Querido Diário API** or INLABS |
| `indicators/` | Quantitative layer already flagged in the README (CEAP anomalies, procurement notices) | Serenata, Querido Diário, FiscalizaJá | Yes — but keep separate and never read as evidence |
| `linkages.yaml` | Graded connections between any signal and any action | — | Manual, by design |

**Balance rule.** Coverage of persons must be driven by the trackers, not the other way round: whoever appears in a Tier 1 action is recorded, across parties and branches — including Centrão figures (emendas parlamentares / "orçamento secreto", ADPF 854), governors, and the justices themselves (Banco Master matter, impeachment requests). Aggregations by party or institution can be reported; per-person "scorecards" should not.

## 4. Automation plan (what is free and keyless)

| Feed | Endpoint | Key | Mirrors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Câmara dos Deputados | `dadosabertos.camara.leg.br/api/v2` — proposições, votações, CPIs | none | `fetch_congress_signals.py` |
| Senado Federal | `legis.senado.leg.br/dadosabertos` — matérias, votações; impeachment requests page | none | same |
| CNJ DataJud | public API (free token published by CNJ) — case movements by process number | public token | new: resolves `official_availability` automatically |
| Portal da Transparência | `api.portaldatransparencia.gov.br` — CEIS, CNEP, CEPIM, sanções | free registration | new: `institutional-actions` |
| Querido Diário | `queridodiario.ok.org.br/api` — gazettes incl. DOU | none | new: DOU keyword scan |
| Federal Register | existing script, add `brazil`, `magnitsky` keywords and Brazil-specific targets | none | already built |
| OFAC / DOJ / BIS | existing agency-releases scraper, filter `brazil`, `brazilian`, `petrobras`, `odebrecht` | none | already built |
| STF news | STF publishes RSS feeds for notícias | none | new: Tier 2 signal |

Each feed writes `data/*.json` and a generated page; a daily digest lists new items; targets in each YAML resolve missing official links. The `tracker.py` dict becomes a thin loader over the YAML for anyone who wants it in Python.

## 5. Site

Migrate from Jekyll/Cayman to MkDocs Material with the `i18n` plugin (EN default, PT, ES), so navigation, search and the three languages are generated from one source. The URL on GitHub Pages does not change. Pages: Home · Methodology (source hierarchy, status taxonomy, linkage grades, presumption of innocence) · Cases · Institutional actions · Legislative signals · Foreign actions · Elections 2026 · Indicators · Linkages · Daily digest · Research and publications (CRS-style reading list: Lava Jato scholarship, Anticorruption Law commentary, TCU/CGU annual reports, foreign reports such as the US State Department human-rights and investment-climate statements).

## 7. Decisions needed before Phase 1

1. **Site generator** — move to MkDocs Material (recommended, matches the other repositories) or stay on Jekyll and generate the three language files from YAML.
2. **Authoring language** — Portuguese for free text with English summaries (recommended; sources are Portuguese), or English throughout.
3. **Person-centred vs matter-centred navigation** — keep sections by person (current) or by tracker with persons as filters (recommended for balance).
4. **Source policy** — confirm that O Antagonista and similar are out, per the README; decide whether Gazeta do Povo and Poder360 count as "mainstream" or "labelled Tier 3".
5. **Repository name** — "political-cases" no longer describes the scope; "brazil-accountability-tracker" (already the working alias) would, with a redirect.
